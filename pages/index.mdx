## Selected Projects

### Energy & Microgrid Analysis

Capstone project for my Master's degree, this project suits itself well for a demonstration because it entirely features open source data.

Analyzed electricity demand, climate conditions, solar exposure, and wind resources across U.S. regions to identify patterns relevant to microgrid planning.

The project uses dimensionality reduction, clustering, and statistical analysis to understand relationships between energy demand and regional conditions.

The system combines computer vision with structured building information to produce predictions at the building level.


## Research & Early Work

Before moving into data science and software engineering, I worked in bioinformatics and genomic sequencing research at Oregon State University. This experience gave me an early foundation in computational biology and working with biological datasets.

Much of my work at the time was assisting researchers with the components of my work, with genomic sequencing data being more and more plentiful and the skills to process them in short supply.

### Genomic Sequencing — *Erwinia billingiae*

Contributed to a whole-genome sequencing project that produced a draft genome sequence for *Erwinia billingiae* OSU19-1, a plant-associated bacterium isolated from a pear tree canker.

The project used **Illumina MiSeq sequencing** and computational genome assembly and annotation. The resulting work was published in *Genome Announcements* in 2015.

**Publication:**
*Klein JM, Bennett RW, MacFarland L, et al. (2015). Draft Genome Sequence of Erwinia billingiae OSU19-1, Isolated from a Pear Tree Canker.*

[Read the publication →](https://journals.asm.org/doi/10.1128/genomea.01119-15)

**My contribution:** Third author and member of the sequencing project team.
